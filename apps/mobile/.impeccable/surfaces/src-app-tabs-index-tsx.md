# Home (and the app-wide look, T23)

Mode: Operate. iPhone first (iOS 26), Android follows. Code-led (no image generation; Operate).

Audience and job: adults in Cairo looking for something new to try and booking it near them; returning learners checking their next session. Constraints: Apple's native tab bar and the floating Liquid Glass buttons stay (owner); SF stays the UI face because Arabic and Dynamic Type need it; no real photos yet (test images only).

Unresolved: real photography (owner), Arabic copy (T21, deferred).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Coach For All in its owner's purple-and-gold world, committed instead of sprinkled: deep aubergine owns the shell (the Home band, selected states, the one action colour), gold marks quality (rating, verified, members), and content is photo-led and compact. It refuses today's arrangement: grey iOS-settings ground with purple as a thin accent and look-alike plain buttons.

OWN-WORLD: white ground; lavender-white surface #FAF9FC for cards with a soft purple-tinted shadow (0 8 24, 6%); lavender tint #F1EAF8 for quiet selection and tiles; ink #14101C, secondary #6E6878, hairline #ECE8F1; aubergine #3B0A6B (pressed #2A0650, dark-mode band #24083F); gold #C9A96E with gold-ink for text. Playfair Display carries display moments (greeting, class and coach names, section titles); SF carries UI. Radii: 20 cards, pills 999. Primary = filled aubergine pill with shadow; secondary = aubergine outline; quiet = tint fill; chips white with hairline, selected filled aubergine.

STORY: open the app, see who you are and what is next in the band, then something new to try below it; one tap to a class, its level visible before tapping, price only at the end.

FIRST VIEWPORT: aubergine band from the status bar down: wordmark left, glass category button right, Playfair greeting in white with a gold dot, secondary line in lavender-white, white search field. The next-session card (only when within 24 h) overlaps the band's lower edge, time set largest. Below on white: Lessons | Groups segmented control, discipline tiles on lavender tint, then the "Classes near you" photo rail. Apple's glass tab bar floats at the bottom.

FORM: owner-pinned world (their prototype) over the roll; roll seed:bc35bfd4a27a0fc7 (assigned Cairo Metro Lines, not taken). Kept raises: the shell colour at full strength, never a pale tint (from the acetate board); one action colour reserved for things you can press (warm consumer app); hierarchy by scale contrast, class name large, instructor small (type specimen); time printed largest on booking cards (gate board). Signature interaction: the Liquid Glass selection lens on segmented controls; motion grammar: springs per motion.lens*, Reduce Motion respected.
